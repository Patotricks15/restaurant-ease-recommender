\section{Dataset}
The Restaurant Consumer Ratings dataset (UCI / Kaggle ref
\texttt{uciml/restaurant-data-with-consumer-ratings}) contains 1{,}161
overall ratings on a 0--2 scale from 138 consumers over 130 restaurants in
Mexico, along with restaurant metadata (cuisine, price tier, ambience).
Ratings carry no event timestamp. Following the implicit-feedback
convention used across this portfolio, a rating of 1 or 2 is treated as a
positive and a rating of 0 is dropped. An iterative $k$-core filter
($k=3$) removes users and restaurants with too little history for
collaborative signal.
