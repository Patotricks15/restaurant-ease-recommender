\section{Experimental protocol}
Because the dataset has no timestamp, we use a \emph{user-aware random
holdout}: for each user with at least $\lceil 1/(1-p)\rceil + 1$ interactions
(where $p=0.3$ is the combined validation+test fraction), 10\% of their
interactions are held out for validation and 20\% for test, sampled
uniformly at random per user; all other users contribute entirely to
training. We report NDCG@10, Recall@10, MAP@10, hit rate, and catalogue
coverage on the test partition, and compare EASE\textsuperscript{R} against
a non-personalised popularity baseline and a random-ranking floor.
