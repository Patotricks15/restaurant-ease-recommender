\section{Methodology}
Let $X \in \{0,1\}^{n \times m}$ be the binary user-item interaction matrix
over $n$ training users and $m$ items. EASE\textsuperscript{R} solves

\begin{equation}
  \min_{B} \; \lVert X - XB \rVert_F^2 + \lambda \lVert B \rVert_F^2,
  \quad \text{s.t.} \; \operatorname{diag}(B) = 0,
  \label{eq:objective}
\end{equation}

where the zero-diagonal constraint prevents the trivial solution $B = I$.
Writing $G = X^\top X + \lambda I$, the Lagrangian stationarity condition
yields the closed-form solution

\begin{align}
  P &= G^{-1}, \label{eq:inverse} \\
  B_{ij} &= \begin{cases}
    -P_{ij} / P_{jj} & i \neq j \\
    0 & i = j
  \end{cases}. \label{eq:weights}
\end{align}

A user's predicted score vector is $\hat{s}_u = x_u B$, where $x_u$ is the
user's own (possibly cold, serving-time) interaction vector: no per-user
embedding is learned, so a user unseen at training time is scored directly
from their observed history. The single hyperparameter $\lambda$ controls
ridge shrinkage of the off-diagonal weights.
